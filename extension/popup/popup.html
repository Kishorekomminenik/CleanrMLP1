What gets captured

  • Nothing is captured until you press Start
  • Screenshots and Full Page are always manual

Export rules

  • ZIP is available after Stop for recordings and screenshots
  • Completed log parts can be downloaded while capture continues
  • WebM = video only
  • ZIP = logs, screenshots (+ video if recorded)

Known limits

  • Full Page may not work on protected or streaming pages
  • Some pages block screenshots by browser design
  • All data stays local to your machine
